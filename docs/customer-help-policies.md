# Customer help and policy content

Implemented locally on 8 September 2026. Public copy is in `app/customer-help.tsx`; contact email, WhatsApp and shipping rates use existing store settings. No external publication or business registration was performed.

## Owner-confirmed facts

- Brand: ZYRA; high-quality fabrics are central to positioning.
- Seven-day size exchange.
- Courier partners supplied by owner: TCS, Protex, Leopards, M&P and Trax. Names are text, not fabricated logos or accreditation.

## Policy choices made under owner's delegation

- Seven calendar days counted from delivery for a voluntary size exchange.
- Unworn and unwashed condition, with tags; replacement subject to stock.
- Customer pays delivery for size preference changes, with costs disclosed before agreement.
- ZYRA covers reasonable return/replacement courier costs for confirmed wrong, damaged or defective goods.
- Defective-goods rights are separate from voluntary size exchanges; no mandatory unboxing video.
- No promised dispatch/refund time invented. Support confirms estimates and refund method in writing.
- Open-parcel availability must be confirmed for the booked courier service.

## Research basis and limits

- Government of Sindh, Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014 (Act XVII of 2015): receipt, return-policy disclosure and misleading representation provisions, sections 19–21. https://www.sindhlaws.gov.pk/setup/publications_SindhCode/PUB-15-000096.pdf
- Competition Commission of Pakistan, deceptive marketing guidance under section 10: avoid unsupported claims and misleading information. https://cc.gov.pk/home/deceptive_marketing
- The Ministry of IT document found for personal-data protection is a consultation draft, not treated as enacted law: https://moitt.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/25821%20DPA%20Bill%20Consultation%20Draft_docx.pdf

This is practical store-policy drafting, not certification of compliance across all Pakistani provinces/territories. Applicable legal requirements depend on the seller and transaction. The seven-day voluntary exchange period is ZYRA's policy, not represented as a nationwide statutory rule.
