## Product-page rule

Each product owns a long-tail query instead of competing with its collection page:

- Oversized tees: `[product name] oversized t shirt Pakistan`
- Bottoms: `[product name] men’s trousers Pakistan`
- Essentials: `[product name] men’s streetwear Pakistan`; use `[product name] polo shirt Pakistan` for polos
- Outerwear/accessories: `[product name] streetwear Pakistan`

Product names, colours, size availability, price and descriptions must remain accurate. Do not add claims such as cotton percentage, GSM, shrink resistance or local manufacture until the product specification confirms them.

## Content opportunities

Create useful editorial pages only after core collection pages are indexed:

1. Oversized T-Shirt Size Guide for Pakistan
2. How an Oversized T-Shirt Should Fit
3. Polo Shirt Fit and Care Guide
4. How to Measure Men’s Trousers at Home

Each guide should link naturally to one relevant collection and the fabric-care page. Avoid near-duplicate city pages and repetitive AI copy.

## Measurement workflow

- Search Console: review queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR and average position every 28 days.
- GA4: measure product views, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase by landing page.
- Promote queries with impressions in positions 4–20; rewrite pages with high impressions but weak CTR.
- Keep winners stable. Change titles or copy only when data shows a clear opportunity.
- Add exact monthly volumes and difficulty only after Google Keyword Planner access is available.
